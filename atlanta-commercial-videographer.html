Most of my clients are 6 and 7 figure brands and the founders behind them: beauty, hair, skincare, lifestyle and luxury companies who need video that looks as premium as the product. That's included Fenty Beauty, Aston Martin and Shea Moisture.

You deal with me the whole way through. I shape the concept, run the shoot and cut the edit, and bring in a crew when the job needs one.

How it works

  2. Quote and planI scope it and send a quote. Once you book, I plan the shoot with a treatment and shot list.

Questions

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted from the brief. Tell me what you need, where it will run and when, and I will send a quote.

Do you work alone or with a crew?

Both. Smaller shoots I handle solo. For bigger productions I bring in a gaffer, grip, 1st AC and PA.

Can you shoot photos on the same day?

Yes. Stills can be added to the video day or booked as their own shoot day, depending on what the project needs.

Are you insured?

Yes.

What do you shoot on?

Sony FX6, FX3 and a7 IV with Sony G Master and Sigma lenses, Nanlite and Amaran lighting, a DJI Ronin and a DJI Mavic for aerials.

What don't you shoot?

I don't take on music videos, weddings or family events. My focus is brands and businesses.

Do you also make creator content?

Yes. I write, shoot and star in content for brands like Microsoft and Charmin. See content creation.

Do you make documentaries?

Yes. Founder stories, brand documentaries and interview-led films. See documentary work.
