About

I'm Tommy, a director and cinematographer based in Atlanta, shooting and editing commercial video for brands.

Most of my work is with beauty, hair and lifestyle brands, and with 6 and 7 figure businesses and entrepreneurs who want their video to look as good as what they sell.

I usually take a job from the first idea to the final edit. On smaller shoots I direct, shoot and edit it myself. On bigger ones I bring in a crew: gaffer, grip, 1st AC and PA. More on that on my commercial video page.

I also make content as a creator, with partners like Microsoft, Western Union and Charmin. See my creator work.
